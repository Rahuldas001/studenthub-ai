StudentHub AI helps you find the places that actually matter when you move to a new campus — where to stay, where to eat, where to study, and what's within walking distance.

REAL PLACES, NOT A CATALOGUE
Every listing comes from OpenStreetMap, so you see the hostel, canteen, pharmacy or ATM that is really there — with its actual address, map position, opening hours and phone number where available.

FIND WHAT'S NEAR YOU
Allow location once and the app centres on the campus nearest you, then sorts everything by distance. Browse by category: Hostels, PGs, Restaurants, Cafes, Mess & tiffin, Libraries, Pharmacies, Gyms, ATMs, Groceries and Bus stops.

SEE IT ON A MAP
A built-in campus map shows every result and your position, so you can judge a walk before you make it.

SAVE AND PLAN
Save the places you like, keep a list of places you want to visit, and revisit what you looked at recently. Everything you save stays on your phone.

FOR OWNERS
Run a PG, hostel or restaurant? Claim your listing, add photos, offers and opening hours, and let students find you.

LOCATIONS
Guwahati, Dibrugarh and Dhubri — more cities as coverage grows.

Notes:
- Location is optional. The app works fully if you deny it; you can pick a city instead.
- Your precise coordinates are used for nearby search only and are never stored on our servers.
- Listings are community-sourced from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Always confirm timings and prices with the owner.
